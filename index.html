The umbrella act empowering the central government to set standards for environmental quality, emissions, and waste discharges.

Thermal Power Emission Standards

Notification setting rigorous limits for coal-fired power stations: PM to 30 mg/Nm³, SO2 to 100-200 mg/Nm³, and NOx to 100 mg/Nm³ based on commissioning dates.

Cement Plant Emission Guidelines

Statutory standards limiting Particulate Matter to 30 mg/Nm³, SO2 to 100 mg/Nm³ (with/without bypass), and NOx to 600-800 mg/Nm³ depending on rotary kiln technologies.

Brick Kiln Standards (2022 Amendment)

Notification mandating the transition of all brick kilns in India to zig-zag chimney technology with a reduced Particulate Matter (PM) limit of 250 mg/Nm³ (FCK) or 50 mg/Nm³ (Zig-zag).

SPCB / CTO-CTE

State Boards & Consent Mechanism (incl. GPCB)

State Pollution Control Boards (GPCB, MPCB, TNPCB, UPPCB, RPCB, KSPCB, WBPCB, APPCB/TSPCB, etc.) enforce CPCB Schedule VI as the statutory floor through Consent to Establish (CTE) and Consent to Operate (CTO). The unit's own consent order is the legally binding limit and can be stricter than the national schedule, especially inside CPCB-notified Critically Polluted Areas (CEPI > 70) — e.g. Gujarat's Vapi, Ankleshwar, Vatva-Narol, Ahmedabad (Naroda/Odhav), Bhavnagar and Junagadh clusters. Always audit against the specific CTO/EC figures, not just the general schedule.

Air (Prevention & Control of Pollution) Act, 1981 GPCB Official Site
Documented Regional Example

CAQM Delhi-NCR: Stricter PM Standard (Real Example)

A concrete, dated example that regional standards genuinely can differ from CPCB Schedule VI: in February 2026 the Commission for Air Quality Management (CAQM) issued a statutory direction setting a uniform particulate matter emission standard of 50 mg/Nm³ for identified industries across Delhi-NCR — stricter than the general CPCB baseline — based on CPCB/IIT-Kanpur technical study. This is exactly the kind of figure that belongs in Manual Limit Override rather than a generic default, since it applies only to specific identified industries in a specific region, not nationwide.

CPCB Schedule VI — Water

General Effluent Discharge Standards (Water)

CPCB's Schedule VI general standards for liquid effluent discharge, under the Water (Prevention & Control of Pollution) Act, 1974 and Environment (Protection) Rules, 1986, vary by receiving medium. For discharge into inland surface water: BOD ≤ 30 mg/l, COD ≤ 250 mg/l, TSS ≤ 100 mg/l. Public sewer discharge is more lenient (e.g. BOD up to 350 mg/l), reflecting lower sensitivity than open water bodies. These are national baseline figures — use Custom Parameters (Water/Effluent category) to check a report's own BOD/COD/TSS/pH readings against them, or against a stricter site-specific CTO figure.
